About CGS Digital Learning

Helping students build practical computer skills through guided learning and hands-on activities.

Learn today. Create for tomorrow.

CGS computer classes provide opportunities for students to understand computers, practise digital tools and express their ideas through documents, spreadsheets, presentations and creative projects.

This website shares learning activities and approved student work with families and the school community.

Learning areas

  • Computer fundamentals
  • MS Paint and creative activities
  • MS Word document skills
  • MS Excel formulas and tables
  • PowerPoint presentations
  • Responsible digital learning
